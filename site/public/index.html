LoomSeal

Proof, not promises.

The KordLoom proof format. One file. Verified by anyone.

Anyone can generate a convincing screenshot now. A log can be edited, a dashboard can lie, and a compliance PDF can be written five minutes before the meeting. When every artifact is cheap to fabricate, the only evidence worth sending is evidence a stranger can check without trusting the sender.

That is what a LoomSeal bundle is: one signed, chained, anchored file. When a KordLoom product says proved, this is the receipt. Verification runs offline, with no account, no server, and no trust in KordLoom or the operator who sent it.

Verify one right now

go install github.com/kordloom/loomseal@latest
git clone https://github.com/kordloom/loomseal
cd loomseal
loomseal verify examples/audit.loomseal.json --evidence examples/evidence
bundle     lsb_example_0001 from loomseal-demo 0.1.0
subject    fleet demo-yard

anchors    1 matched by coordinates, 0 proofs carried, not validated in this version

VERIFIED   signed, chained (full), anchored by reference

What a verified bundle means

  • The producer holding the signing key assembled these exact claims. Flip one byte and the signature fails, loudly, with the break named.
  • The claims sit in an append-only chain that has not been reordered, rewritten, or trimmed since its heads were anchored outside the producer's reach. Anchored history cannot be manufactured after the fact. Whoever has been recording, wins.
  • Every artifact you were handed matches its recorded digest exactly: the page as it stood, the run as it happened, the approval as it was given.

And the boundary, stated plainly: a chain fixes the record, not the character of the recorder. The conformance wording is exactly three words: signed, chained, anchored. No other adjectives, on purpose.
